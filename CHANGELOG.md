# Changelog

## 1.0.0, for Minecraft 1.21.1

- Reel n Creel now also runs on Minecraft 1.21.1, on both Fabric and NeoForge, with the same fish, rods, fight, foods,
  buckets, breeding, treasure and fishermen as on the newer versions. It needs Java 21.
- Bundles are an experiment in Minecraft 1.21.1, so catching a fish straight into a bundle works only in worlds made with
  the Bundles experiment switched on. Shulker boxes and water buckets work in every world.

## 1.0.0, for Minecraft 26.3, 26.2, and 26.1, 26.1.1 and 26.1.2

- The first full release. Reel n Creel now runs on Minecraft 26.3, 26.2, and 26.1, 26.1.1 and 26.1.2, on both Fabric and
  NeoForge, with the same features on each: the same fish, rods, fight, foods, buckets, breeding, treasure and fishermen. Pick
  the file made for your Minecraft version and your mod loader. The 26.1 file serves all three of 26.1, 26.1.1 and 26.1.2.
- The mod's fish foods cook in a smoker exactly as fast as vanilla's fish do in your version of the game.

## 0.24.1, for Minecraft 26.3

- The Northern Pike, Muskie, Alligator Gar and Barracuda have reshaped heads. Their mouths no longer run flat along the
  bottom to the tip: the chin now curves up under the snout. The pike, muskie and gar have a broad snout rising to the skull
  instead of a long flat one, and the barracuda's lower jaw juts past the upper. Nothing else about these fish changes.

## 0.24.0, for Minecraft 26.3

- Vanilla's Fishing Rod, Carrot on a Stick and Warped Fungus on a Stick have a new look in the hand: a dark wooden pole with
  iron bands and guides, a small white reel under it, and a flat pixel line looping from guide to guide down to a red and white
  bobber with a hook, a carrot or a warped fungus. In the inventory, on the ground and in item frames they keep vanilla's
  pictures, and they work exactly as before.
- When you cast vanilla's Fishing Rod, its line now leaves the rod's tip guide and is drawn as the mod's rods' line is, with the
  mod's 3D bobber. Fishing rods from other mods keep their own line and bobber.
- A resource pack that restyles these three items still wins over the mod's look.

## 0.23.0, for Minecraft 26.3

- Breeding is now something you watch. Feed two fish of the same kind with Seagrass and they find each other (within 8
  blocks), swim together, rise to just under the surface, circle each other with hearts for a few seconds, and lay their Fish
  Spawn on the surface above. A pair that cannot reach each other still lays after 15 seconds. If one of a pair is caught,
  bucketed or killed, the other stays ready to breed.
- Fish that can breed follow you while you hold Seagrass in either hand, stopping a little short of you, and lose interest
  when you put it away. Giants and vanilla's fish do not.
- No more thin line between Fish Spawn and its soft edge when you look at it from low down or with shaders: the edge now meets
  the spawn exactly.
- Fish Spawn has one item, for creative mode: the mod's tab has a stack of each kind ("Brook Trout Spawn" and so on), placed on
  water as frogspawn is, and pick-block on spawn gives its stack. It still drops nothing and cannot be had in survival.

## 0.22.0, for Minecraft 26.3

- The mod's fish can be bred. Use Seagrass on a fish to feed it: hearts show and it is ready to breed for 30 seconds. Then feed
  another fish of the same kind within 8 blocks of it, and the two pair at once.
- A pair lays Fish Spawn on the water's surface nearby, a patch of jelly with eggs in it, coloured by the kind of fish (orange,
  amber, cream, clear or green). After a while (3 to 10 minutes, as frogspawn) it hatches into 1 to 3 small fish of that kind,
  which stay small and never despawn.
- Fish Spawn behaves as frogspawn does: it lies only on water, breaks at a touch and drops nothing. It has no item and cannot be
  picked up. Spawn laid next to spawn runs together into one patch.
- If there is no open water surface near the pair (under ice or a roof), no spawn is laid and a puff of smoke shows it.
- After pairing, each fish must wait 5 minutes before it can be fed again. A fish already fed, a fish on your line and the
  giants (the sturgeon, the tunas, the sailfish, the tarpon and the alligator gar) cannot be fed: the seagrass is kept. Giants
  never breed. Vanilla's fish are unchanged.

## 0.21.1, for Minecraft 26.3

- The Ol' Mariner's cane tip and cork grip are a warmer colour.

## 0.21.0, for Minecraft 26.3

- A sixth rod, the Ol' Mariner: an old wooden rod of walnut and cane with a worn cork grip, brass rings and a wooden reel. It
  catches fish of every size, from the smallest to the giants, wherever any fish would bite: mostly the smaller ones, now and
  then a big one. Luck of the Sea tilts it toward the bigger fish. Each fish fights as it does on any other rod.
- It lasts 224 uses, casts as far as the vanilla Fishing Rod, takes the same enchantments as the other rods, and brings a
  treasure with a landed fish 3.5% of the time.
- Crafted (no pattern) from a Fishing Rod, a Nautilus Shell and an Angler Pottery Sherd. It comes into the recipe book with the
  other rods.
- The other five rods are unchanged.

## 0.20.0, for Minecraft 26.3

- The bobber of the mod's rods is now solid: vanilla's bobber in three dimensions, drawn with the game's own bobber texture.
  The vanilla Fishing Rod's bobber is unchanged.

## 0.19.2, for Minecraft 26.3

- The rod's own sounds (the creak, the click, the reel's tick) are louder.

## 0.19.1, for Minecraft 26.3

- The rod's sounds (the creak, the click, the reel) are now heard when you fish from a boat or stand in water with the mod
  Sound Physics Remastered installed. They were muffled away because they came from the player's feet, under the waterline.

## 0.19.0, for Minecraft 26.3

- Hold a Water Bucket in your other hand when you land a fish, and the fish goes straight into the bucket, alive, instead of
  flopping at your feet. This works for a vanilla salmon on the line too.
- Hold a Shulker Box or a Bundle in your other hand instead, and the fish goes in as its meat, as if you had killed it, with the
  experience. Whatever does not fit is thrown to you.
- So you can now fish from a boat at sea: a landed fish no longer falls back into the water.
- One Raw Fish crafts into two Fish Morsels (there is no recipe back).
- The fight, the rods and the treasure are unchanged.

## 0.18.0, for Minecraft 26.3

- A NeoForge build, beside the Fabric one. Both come from the same code and play the same.
- The mod's entry in the mod list now links to the source and the issue tracker.
- No change to fish, rods or fishing.

## 0.17.1 (beta), for Minecraft 26.3

The first public release.

- 53 fish modelled on real species, each appearing in the water that suits it, in four sizes.
- Fish meat (Fish Morsel, Raw Fish and their cooked forms), one bucket for every fish, and a spawn egg for each.
- Five fishing rods, each hooking live fish of two size classes, crafted from a vanilla Fishing Rod.
- A fight read from the bend of the rod: hold use to reel, let go to give line.
- A chance of treasure with a landed fish, and two extra fisherman trades that buy the catch.
- The vanilla Fishing Rod is unchanged.

Known gaps: multiplayer has not been tested yet.
