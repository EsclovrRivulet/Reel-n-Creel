# Changelog

## 0.18.0, for Minecraft 26.3

- A NeoForge build, beside the Fabric one. Both come from the same code and play the same.
- The mod's entry in the mod list now links to the source and the issue tracker.
- No change to fish, rods or fishing.

## 0.17.1 (beta), for Minecraft 26.3

The first public release.

- 53 fish modelled on real species, each appearing in the water that suits it, in four sizes.
- Fish meat (Fish Morsel, Raw Fish and their cooked forms), one bucket for every fish, and a spawn egg for each.
- Five fishing rods, each hooking live fish of two size classes, crafted from a vanilla Fishing Rod.
- A fight read from the bend of the rod: hold use to reel, let go to give line.
- A chance of treasure with a landed fish, and two extra fisherman trades that buy the catch.
- The vanilla Fishing Rod is unchanged.

Known gaps: multiplayer has not been tested yet.
